About the studio

AissaGames is an independent studio focused on mobile games that are easy to learn and deep to master. Every game is designed for phones first, supports multiple languages, and is built with a clear privacy policy and a simple way for players to delete their data.

Our games

Kharbga

A faithful digital edition of the traditional North African strategy board game. Place your pieces, capture your opponent by surrounding them, and outplay the AI or a friend. Features a premium board, collectible piece skins and online play.

Learn more · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Onet Things

A relaxing match-and-connect puzzle with 160 hand-built levels, themed worlds and backgrounds, and 17 supported languages. Connect two matching objects with a path of no more than two turns and clear the board before time runs out.

Learn more · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Mindlock

A color code-breaking puzzle. Deduce the hidden sequence from your clues in as few attempts as possible, with progressively harder levels and 17 supported languages.
